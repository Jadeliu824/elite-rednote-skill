# Bad Examples

Anti-patterns to reject. Each is a real failure mode with the fix.

## 1. Summary disguised as content

> **Title:** Figma 和 Adobe 的并购交易详解
> 2022 年 Adobe 宣布收购 Figma，交易金额约 200 亿美元。随后英国 CMA 和欧盟对此展开调查，认为该交易可能损害竞争。2023 年 12 月双方宣布终止交易。

**Why it fails:** a competent summarizer of the same source produces exactly this. No claim, no tension, no judgment. It is aggregation, not ELITE content.

**Fix:** return to Stage 3 and find the argument (see good example).

## 2. AI-sounding copy

> 在当今时代，随着设计行业的不断发展，协作工具的重要性日益凸显。值得注意的是，Figma 的成功体现了一种以用户为中心的产品理念，这本质上是底层逻辑的胜利。总而言之，创业者应该不断迭代自己的认知，从而实现商业闭环。

**Why it fails:** every banned pattern in one paragraph ("在当今时代""值得注意的是""体现了""本质上""底层逻辑""迭代""闭环""总而言之"). It reads like a machine, says nothing, and ends in an empty uplift.

**Fix:** run the 去 AI 味 pass (`editorial-guidelines.md` §5.1). Rewrite as a person would say it: "Figma 赢的地方很具体：让设计师和工程师在同一个文件里干活。"

## 3. Keyword stuffing

> **标题:** 创始人决策 商业案例 并购案例 产品战略 Figma Adobe 收购失败 创业干货 认知升级 思维模型 深度思考
> #创始人 #商业 #案例 #决策 #战略 #认知 #思维 #模型 #干货 #创业 #职场 #成长 #学习

**Why it fails:** a wall of tags, a title that is a keyword dump, and abstract tags with no topic. Reads as spam, kills search intent, and violates the platform rule of natural phrasing.

**Fix:** one readable title + 2-5 natural search phrases + 3-8 relevant hashtags.

## 4. 标题党

> **标题:** 那个拒绝 200 亿的男人，把 Adobe 狠狠打脸了 🔥

**Why it fails:** the post does not deliver "打脸"; it is emotional bait that the content cannot back. Violates the "titles promise exactly what the post delivers" rule and erodes trust.

**Fix:** state the real claim plainly.

## 5. Off-brand visual

> **封面:** 用金色描边的奢华边框 + 大理石纹理背景 + 一堆 emoji 贴纸，标题放在一条金色丝带上，字号很小。

**Why it fails:** cheap-luxury cliché, decoration over hierarchy, small headline. Violates Midnight Sky, the split-page structure, and the "one dominant headline" rule.

**Fix:** Midnight Sky `#16151D` background, image-led upper field, one large warm-white headline, a single restrained accent.

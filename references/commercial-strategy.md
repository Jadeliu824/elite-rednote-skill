# ELITE Commercial Strategy

How ELITE content acquires readers, builds trust, converts the knowledge base, and discovers future products — without sacrificing content quality. Load before Stage 1 (topic discovery), Stage 6 (optimization), and Stage 8 (performance review).

## 1. The commercial goal

The account exists to do four things, in order:

1. Attract potential buyers of the existing Notion knowledge base.
2. Build trust through consistent content, accumulating leads for future digital products.
3. Identify reader demand, giving real market validation for new products.
4. Build a sustainable content → product → repeat-purchase system.

**Core principle:** 免费内容证明创作者的能力，付费产品出售系统性价值.

Never force a purchase by truncating a post, hiding the conclusion, or manufacturing information asymmetry. Free notes must have independent reading value; paid products offer a more complete system, deeper research, tools, and application.

## 2. Three product tiers

### Tier 1 — Free (小红书 notes)
Purpose: attention, trust, cognitive value. Every note provides a real question/topic, a valuable core view, necessary facts/cases/data/reasoning, and a complete takeaway — plus a natural onward path. Never omit the core conclusion to drive sales.

### Tier 2 — Paid (ELITE Notion knowledge base)
Purpose: systematic knowledge value. Contains complete frameworks, multi-angle research, curated sources and cases, reusable methods/templates/tools, and cross-topic connections — maintained and updated. **Advertise only what actually exists** in the knowledge base; never invent a table of contents, case, feature, or update promise.

### Tier 3 — Future (independent digital products)
Purpose: solve a specific problem the user can act on — playbooks, decision tools, templates/workflows, special-topic research, learning/practice systems. The skill may *identify* demand for these (from reader feedback), but must **never describe an unbuilt product as existing**.

## 3. Four content roles

Every topic gets a primary commercial role (recorded in the topic brief). Initial mix is a hypothesis, not a quota — adjust with data.

| Role | Goal | Typical topics | CTA priority |
|------|------|----------------|--------------|
| **A. Traffic (流量)** | attract new readers | global trends, social phenomena, tech shifts, hot business topics, debatable public issues | 关注 / 收藏 / 讨论 — often none |
| **B. Trust (信任)** | prove research & judgment | capital-market analysis, business case teardowns, deep viewpoints, evidence-backed analysis | 延伸阅读 / 查看专题 / 关注后续 |
| **C. Product (产品)** | show the knowledge base's real value | real catalog, topic showcases, framework demos, template/tool demos, use cases, iteration logs | 了解知识库 / 查看详情 |
| **D. Conversion (转化)** | drive actual purchase | why ELITE exists, what it solves, what's inside, who it's for, samples, real updates | 明确引导购买 |

Initial mix: **Traffic 30% · Trust 35% · Product 20% · Conversion 15%.** Adjust from actual data, never treat as a fixed quota.

## 4. Product Bridge (every note)

After research and before writing, check whether this piece has a **real, natural** connection to the paid product. Answer:

1. Which commercial role does this note serve?
2. Which pillar (WORLD/BUSINESS/CAPITAL/BUILD)?
3. Does the knowledge base actually contain a related topic or content?
4. What would the reader want to know *next* after the free note?
5. What systematic value can the paid product offer that a free note shouldn't carry?
6. What CTA (if any)?

**Principles:**
- Never insert an ad awkwardly. Never repeat the same sales line every note.
- Never force a link to an unrelated topic.
- Never claim the knowledge base has content it doesn't.
- Lead naturally from the reader's next learning need.
- If there is no real product connection, **do no direct sales CTA** — write "无直接产品关联".

**Forbidden:** "内容不再展开，完整版请购买知识库."

**Recommended direction:** "这篇笔记讨论的是价格与价值的基本区别。关于估值逻辑、现金流和安全边际，我在 ELITE 中整理了更系统的研究内容。" — and only after verifying that content really exists.

## 5. Note structure (when applicable)

Hook → Insight → Evidence → Takeaway → Product Bridge → CTA. Not a rigid template:

- Traffic content may omit product promotion entirely.
- Trust content centers on establishing recognition.
- Product content must show concrete product value.
- Conversion content must state a clear reason to buy.

## 6. Content ↔ product mapping

| Pillar | Free direction | Paid extension |
|--------|----------------|----------------|
| WORLD | global trends, tech, culture, society | topic research, trend analysis, source curation |
| BUSINESS | business models, companies, marketing, strategy | business-analysis frameworks, case studies |
| CAPITAL | markets, investment logic, finance, wealth | investment research system, topic materials |
| BUILD | personal practice, product building, systems | workflows, templates, hands-on tools |

This is a mapping framework — not all of it exists yet. Cross-reference `product-inventory.md` for what is real.

## 7. Conversion funnel

小红书笔记 → 主页访问 → 主页置顶内容 → 产品展示页 → 购买知识库 → 未来产品交叉销售与复购.

Support with **three pinned notes** (长期置顶):

1. **ELITE 是什么** — positioning, content philosophy, why it exists.
2. **ELITE 里面有什么** — real catalog, content screenshots, delivery samples.
3. **谁适合购买 ELITE** — target reader, use cases, value after purchase.

Also check, and keep checking: bio expresses positioning; product entry is clear; product page shows real content; purchase flow is smooth; description matches delivery.

**Platform rules:** 小红书 external-link, 导流, and 交易 rules can change. Every conversion path must obey the current rules — do not assume off-site links or 私信导流 are always allowed. Verify the latest rules when needed and design an allowed alternative path.

## 8. Commercial metrics

Track in Stage 8 (`workflows/performance-review.md`):

| Metric | What it tells you |
|--------|-------------------|
| 阅读量 | topic + cover appeal |
| 收藏率 | durable content value |
| 主页访问率 | desire to know more |
| 商品点击率 | product appeal |
| 咨询率 | purchase intent |
| 成交率 | product/price/trust fit |

Rules: separate traffic performance from commercial performance; never treat likes as the sole success signal; record each note's role, CTA, and linked product; compare which roles drive 主页访问 / 商品点击 / 成交; never fabricate backend data (mark "待采集" when missing). Watch the chain **主页访问率 × 商品点击率 × 成交率**, not just 阅读量.

## 9. Demand & product-opportunity discovery

In the performance review, collect real signals:

- real questions in comments and DMs;
- difficulties readers raise repeatedly;
- topics where saves and discussion cluster;
- questions about the existing knowledge base;
- problems readers are willing to pay to solve;
- purchase signals already shown.

Evaluate each candidate opportunity:

1. Is the demand real? 2. Does it recur? 3. Is there a clear audience? 4. Is there willingness-to-pay evidence? 5. What is the cost to build and maintain? 6. Can it become a repeatable sale?

Never conclude demand exists just from the creator's own interest.

## 10. Relationship to the editorial voice

Editorial notes (Traffic/Trust) keep the brand's restraint — the Product Bridge is a natural pointer, never a cliffhanger or "完整版请购买". Only Product/Conversion content uses an explicit purchase CTA, and always honestly, always referencing content that `product-inventory.md` confirms exists.

# Stage 8 — Performance Review

After publishing, read the numbers and convert them into better decisions. Do not chase traffic blindly; treat metrics as three separate layers (see `references/platform-guidelines.md` §5).

## 1. Collect

Gather what is available: impressions, views, CTR (when the platform exposes it), likes, saves, comments, shares, profile visits, and any leads/conversions. Record the date range and, when possible, the first few days vs. later — Xiaohongshu search can make a post grow late.

## 2. Classify

Sort each metric into its layer:

- **Reach** — impressions, views, CTR.
- **Engagement** — likes, saves, comments, shares.
- **Business outcome** — profile visits, leads, conversions (directional for ELITE today).

## 3. Diagnose

- Reach low, engagement normal → the cover/title failed to earn the open. Fix packaging next cycle.
- Reach high, saves low → the argument did not deliver durable value. Sharpen the angle, not the packaging.
- Saves high, comments low → strong utility; not a failure.
- A low-traffic post with high saves may be a better post than a high-traffic post with low saves.

## 4. Convert to a decision

Every finding becomes a concrete, forward-looking change:

- A topic pattern that over-performed → add a note to Stage 1 to seek more like it.
- A title/cover pattern that under-performed → a specific packaging rule for next time.
- A pillar or layer that consistently outperforms → shift the mix.

Write the learnings down where they will be seen: append to the running record for the next `topic-discovery` pass. The loop closes here.

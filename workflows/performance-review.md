# Stage 8 — Performance Review

After publishing, read the numbers and convert them into better decisions — both editorial and commercial. Do not chase traffic blindly; do not treat likes as the only success signal. Strategy reference: `references/commercial-strategy.md`.

## 1. Collect

Gather what is available; mark anything missing as **待采集** (do not fabricate backend data). Record each note's **content role, CTA, and linked product** so results can be compared across roles.

- **Traffic:** impressions, views, CTR (when exposed), likes, saves, comments, shares, profile visits.
- **Commercial:** 阅读量, 收藏率, 主页访问率, 商品点击率, 咨询率, 成交率.

Note the date range and early-vs-late behavior — 小红书 search can make a post grow late.

## 2. Classify

Sort into three layers (see `references/platform-guidelines.md` §5):

- **Reach** — impressions, views, CTR.
- **Engagement** — likes, saves, comments, shares (saves >> likes for evergreen value).
- **Business outcome** — profile visits, 商品点击, 咨询, 成交.

## 3. Diagnose

- Reach low, engagement normal → cover/title failed to earn the open. Fix packaging.
- Reach high, saves low → the argument did not deliver durable value. Sharpen the angle, not the packaging.
- Saves high, comments low → strong utility; not a failure.
- **Commercial focus:** watch the chain 主页访问率 × 商品点击率 × 成交率. A high-阅读量 note that drives no profile/product visits is traffic, not business.

## 4. Convert to a decision

Every finding becomes a forward change:

- Topic/cover pattern that over-performed → note in Stage 1 to seek more.
- A content role that drove more 主页访问 / 商品点击 / 成交 → shift the mix (`commercial-strategy.md` §3) and adjust CTA posture.
- A title/cover pattern that under-performed → a specific packaging rule.

Write learnings where they will be seen: append to the running record for the next `topic-discovery` pass.

## 5. Demand & product-opportunity discovery

From comments, DMs, saves clustering, and purchase signals, collect real evidence of demand:

- real questions and repeated difficulties;
- topics where saves/discussion cluster;
- questions about the existing knowledge base;
- problems readers are willing to pay to solve;
- purchase signals already shown.

Evaluate each candidate opportunity against the six criteria in `references/commercial-strategy.md` §9 (real? recurring? clear audience? willingness-to-pay evidence? cost? repeatable?). Record opportunities in the product pipeline; do not claim demand from the creator's own interest alone.

The loop closes here: editorial learnings → Stage 1 topic discovery; commercial learnings → content-role mix and product development.

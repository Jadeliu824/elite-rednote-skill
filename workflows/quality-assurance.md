# Stage 7 — Quality Assurance

Run before delivering *anything*. This is a gate, not a checklist to skim. Three layers: the nine editorial checks, the 100-point score, and the commercial checks. Weak output is revised or rejected — never shipped.

## 1. The nine editorial checks

1. **Factual accuracy** — every date, number, name, quote verified? Any unsupported claim present?
2. **Source quality** — claims trace to primary/reliable secondary sources? Recorded in `research-notes`?
3. **Argument quality** — a real, disagreeable central argument, or a summary in disguise?
4. **Originality** — would a competent summarizer of the same source produce this?
5. **Reader value** — does the reader leave with signal they did not have? Is the takeaway usable?
6. **Narrative quality** — strong opening, clear progression, natural ending? No AI transitions, no bullet fragmentation, no forced conclusion?
7. **Brand consistency** — 局内人/判断力 voice, 具体 > 模糊 > 抽象, no engagement-begging, no 标题党? Pillar/layer/role correct?
8. **Visual consistency** — Midnight Sky `#16151D`, correct tokens, split-page cover, one dominant headline, restrained accent? (Once a visual exists.)
9. **Platform suitability** — compliant, title/cover honest, search terms natural?

## 2. The 100-point score

Score every piece; give a concrete reason per dimension and name what most needs fixing. Scoring is evidence, not ritual.

| Dimension | Points |
|-----------|-------:|
| 开头吸引力 | 15 |
| 核心观点与独立判断 | 20 |
| 事实、案例与信息密度 | 15 |
| 逻辑与论证质量 | 15 |
| 叙事与阅读节奏 | 15 |
| 语言自然度与品牌辨识度 | 10 |
| 结尾与读者记忆点 | 5 |
| 商业关联自然度 | 5 |
| **Total** | **100** |

Thresholds:

- **< 85** — must revise.
- **85–89** — may enter human review.
- **≥ 90** — meets suggested-publish standard.

**Hard fails** — these override the score: a hollow core argument, a factual error, a weak opening, or visibly templated language. If any fires, the piece does not pass regardless of total.

## 3. Commercial checks

When the piece has a Product Bridge or CTA (or should have one), verify:

- Does the free content have independent value?
- Is any key conclusion deliberately hidden?
- Is the evidence credible enough?
- Is the linked paid product accurately described (per `references/product-inventory.md`)?
- Is there a real reason given for the further purchase?
- Is the CTA natural and matched to the content role?
- Is the ad language repetitive/mechanical?
- Does it obey current 小红书 rules (links, 导流, 交易)?
- Any fabricated product feature or delivery promise?
- Can the commercial result be tracked after publishing?

A thin free value, a forced product link, or a fake promise → do not pass.

## 4. Verdict

- **All three layers pass** → deliver.
- **1-2 localizable defects** → fix the specific defect and re-check. Do not rewrite the whole piece for a local issue.
- **Argument / Originality / Reader-value fails, or any hard fail fires** → the piece is weak at the core. Return to Stage 3 (or Stage 1) with the diagnosis; do not polish it into shipping shape.

**Honesty rule:** if you cannot say a check passes without stretching, it does not pass. Report the defect plainly.

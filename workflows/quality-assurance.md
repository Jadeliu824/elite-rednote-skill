# Stage 7 — Quality Assurance

Run before delivering *anything*. This is a gate, not a checklist to skim. Weak output is revised or rejected — never shipped.

## 1. The nine checks

For each check, the answer is PASS or a specific defect.

1. **Factual accuracy** — every date, number, name, and quote verified against a source? Any unsupported claim still present?
2. **Source quality** — claims trace to primary/reliable secondary sources (not rumor, not fabrication)? Sources recorded in `research-notes`?
3. **Argument quality** — is there a real, disagreeable central argument, or is it a summary in disguise?
4. **Originality** — would a competent summarizer of the same source produce this? (If yes, it fails.)
5. **Reader value** — does the reader leave with signal they did not have? Is the takeaway usable?
6. **Narrative quality** — strong opening, clear progression, natural ending? No AI-style transitions, no bullet fragmentation, no forced conclusion?
7. **Brand consistency** — 局内人/判断力 voice, 具体 > 模糊 > 抽象, no CTA, no 标题党? Pillar and layer correctly assigned?
8. **Visual consistency** — Midnight Sky `#16151D` background, correct tokens, split-page cover, one dominant headline, restrained accent? (Applies once a visual spec exists.)
9. **Platform suitability** — compliant with Xiaohongshu rules, title/cover honest to content, search terms natural (no stuffing)?

## 2. Verdict

- **All nine PASS** → deliver.
- **1-2 defects, localizable** → fix the specific defect (rewrite the phrase, re-verify the number, re-token the color) and re-check. Do not rewrite the whole piece for a local issue.
- **Argument/Originality/Reader-value fails** → the piece is weak at the core. Do not polish it. Return to Stage 3 (or Stage 1) with the diagnosis.

## 3. Honesty rule

If you cannot say a check passes without stretching, it does not pass. Report the defect plainly rather than approving a weak deliverable. ELITE's reputation is the asset this gate protects.

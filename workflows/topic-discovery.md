# Stage 1 — Topic Discovery

Find and commit to a topic worth producing. The goal is not a list of ideas; it is a *decision* with a rationale.

## 1. Gather candidates

Pull candidates from a mix of:

- **Fresh developments** in WORLD / BUSINESS / CAPITAL (a decision, a number, a shift, a filing) from the last 1-2 weeks.
- **Strong sources** whose recent output contains extractable judgment (founder interviews, investor essays, teardowns, primary data).
- **Reader questions** — recurring "why / how / what changed" questions in the target audience.
- **Your own build** (BUILD pillar) when something real happened with a transferable lesson.

Two or three candidates is enough to decide among; a dozen is noise.

## 2. Score each candidate

Score 1-5 on each dimension. A score of 5 means "unmistakably strong," 3 "adequate," 1 "weak."

| Dimension | Question | Weight |
|-----------|----------|--------|
| **Originality** | Would someone else summarize this source the same way? (high = no) | 25% |
| **Relevance** | Does the target reader care *now*? Does it touch their decisions? | 20% |
| **Information value** | How much signal does the reader get that they did not already have? | 25% |
| **Discussion potential** | Is there something to argue with — tension, a counterintuitive claim? | 15% |
| **Brand fit** | Does it sit in a pillar and let ELITE speak with judgment? | 15% |

Weighted score = Σ(dimension × weight). Thresholds:

- **≥ 4.0** — strong, proceed.
- **3.0 - 3.9** — acceptable only if no better candidate exists; note what is weak.
- **< 3.0** — reject. Repetitive, obvious, or brand-mismatched topics die here.

## 3. Freshness check

If the topic is tied to a development, confirm the date. An old source is allowed only when the *judgment* is what is fresh — and then you say so in the brief ("源为 7 月，选它因判断张力优先"), not hide it.

## 4. Assign the content role

Give the topic a primary commercial role (Traffic / Trust / Product / Conversion) per `references/commercial-strategy.md` §3. This sets the CTA posture and whether a Product Bridge is expected: Traffic content may skip promotion entirely; Product/Conversion content must only reference real, inventoried product content.

## 5. Decide and record

Choose the single best candidate. Reject the rest. Fill `templates/topic-brief.md` with:

- The topic, one pillar, the layer (常青/时效/日记), and the **content role**.
- The five scores and the weighted result.
- The one-line rationale for *why this one*.
- Any known weakness to watch in later stages.

The brief is the contract the rest of the pipeline works against. If a later stage cannot make the topic work, return here rather than forcing it.
